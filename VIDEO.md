# 3-minute English video script

Record on YouTube or Vimeo, public, **under 3:00**. No copyrighted music. No Ring trademarks as if Kinwatch were an Amazon product. Say **Ring app** / **Ring Appstore** when needed. Show Playground or a real device **on screen**. Cut at 2:50. Captions on.

Human records this. Agents do not upload.

## 0:00–0:20 Cold open

Night. Companion (large type): **“Mom’s front door opened at 2:11. No person on camera. Door still open.”**
Cut doorbell fixture + contact-open in the timeline.

## 0:20–0:50 Show me

Family: **“Show me.”**
MCP App live view widget + privacy-safe snapshot card. Vision line: **“Empty porch. Door ajar.”**
No device IDs.

## 0:50–1:20 I’m looking

Family: **“Tell the house I’m looking.”**
Chime playback. Policy chip: escalate if still open in 2 minutes.

## 1:20–1:50 Overnight

Morning. **“What happened overnight?”**
Timeline widget: fused doorbell + door + empty porch. Caretaking recap, not a DVR dump.

## 1:50–2:20 Same server

Quick cut: `tools/list` on `/mcp` (protocol 2025-11-25) and the AgentCore listen address. One sentence: same tools as the voice host.

## 2:20–2:50 Two personas

Large-type / voice for the person at home. Remote family timeline. One line on Ring Appstore as a private-app distribution path — not a certification claim.

## 2:50–3:00 Stop

Black. Repo URL.
